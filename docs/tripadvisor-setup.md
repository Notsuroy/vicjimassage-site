## Step 1: Create the listing

1. Go to **tripadvisor.com/Owners**
2. Click **List your property free**
3. Choose **Things to Do / Attraction** (NOT Hotel, NOT Restaurant)
4. Category path: **Spas & Wellness** then **Spas**

## Step 2: Verification

TripAdvisor verifies ownership before the listing goes live.

- Phone verification: they call the number listed and read a code
- Email verification: sent to the owner account email
- Manual moderation: a human reviews new attraction listings

Expect 5 to 15 business days. Do not create a second listing if the first has not appeared yet. Duplicate listings get merged or removed and slow everything down.

### Note on one omitted claim

The site and the Google Business Profile description both carry the line "Sou o único no Brasil oferecendo esse trabalho integrado" (I am the only one in Brazil offering this integrated work).

That line is **deliberately left out of the TripAdvisor version**. TripAdvisor moderates superlative and unverifiable claims more aggressively than Google does, and an unprovable "only one in Brazil" statement is a common reason new attraction listings get held or rejected. The rest of the description carries the same positioning without the risk.

If Victor wants the claim included, it is his call. Just know it raises the odds of a moderation hold on a listing that otherwise sails through.

## Step 6: The first reviews (the hard part)

A TripAdvisor listing with zero reviews does not surface in category browsing. This is the single blocker between a live listing and a ranking one.

TripAdvisor reviews are harder to collect than Google reviews because the reviewer needs a TripAdvisor account and typically reviews things they did while traveling. Locals rarely use the platform.

**Target the right people.** Ask clients who were **visiting** Itacaré, not locals. International travelers first, then Brazilians from other cities.

**Target number:** 6 reviews puts him at #1 in the category by review count. Aim for 8 to 10 to be safe.

**Important compliance note:** Never offer a discount, free session, or anything of value in exchange for a review. TripAdvisor and Google both treat incentivized reviews as fraud and will remove reviews and penalize the listing. Ask plainly.

### WhatsApp request, English

```
Hi [Name], hope you're doing well and that the body is still feeling good from the session.

I just set up a TripAdvisor listing so travelers coming to Itacaré can find the work. Would you be willing to leave a short review of your session? It genuinely helps other visitors find me.

Here's the direct link: [TRIPADVISOR REVIEW LINK]

No pressure at all, and thank you either way. It was a pleasure working with you.

Victor
```

### WhatsApp request, Portuguese

```
Oi [Nome], espero que esteja tudo bem e que o corpo ainda esteja leve da sessão.

Acabei de criar um perfil no TripAdvisor para que viajantes que vêm para Itacaré consigam encontrar o trabalho. Você toparia deixar uma avaliação curta da sua sessão? Ajuda bastante outros visitantes a me encontrarem.

O link direto é esse: [LINK DE AVALIAÇÃO DO TRIPADVISOR]

Sem nenhuma pressão, e obrigado de qualquer forma. Foi um prazer te atender.

Victor
```

The review link becomes available in the owner dashboard once the listing is approved. It looks like `tripadvisor.com/UserReviewEdit-d[LISTING_ID]`.
